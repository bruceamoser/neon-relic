# The Heavenly Crucifix

## The call

An artifact designated "The Crucifix of St. Vitus" has been stolen from the Prague Castle chapel museum. The theft was not discovered by local authorities. A forgery was substituted. A Covenant routine audit detected an anomalous Corruption signature in Vienna, triggering this case. The artifact is an iron crucifix historically associated with Count Jaroslav Bořita of Martinice, who survived the 1618 Defenestration of Prague. Covenant analysis suggests the survival was not miraculous. The crucifix possesses properties that render its wearer immune to bodily harm.

## If the team does nothing

A forgery already hangs in Prague. In Vienna the signal returns when Klaus Reiner wears the original. His conviction that nothing can hurt him grows with every demonstration. Cardinal Voss offers help while quietly closing other avenues. Within ten days the Compact expects the buyer at the Bavarian hunting lodge. Countess Martinice’s letters point to the older shrine, giving the team a route independent of the sale.

## Behind the incident

The Crucifix of St. Vitus is not a Christian relic. The iron was smelted from a meteorite that struck the Bohemian Forest around 900 CE, witnessed by a Slavic pagan community who built a shrine at the impact site. They believed the iron was a fragment of the sky-god's armor. When Christianity arrived, the shrine was demolished, the iron was reforged into a crucifix, and the pagan origin was buried under hagiography. The effect (immunity to bodily harm) is intrinsic to the meteoric iron. It is not divine. It is material that does not belong on Earth. The property activates when the iron is worn against skin.

Six weeks ago, a professional crew stole the crucifix from the Prague Castle chapel museum, replacing it with an exceptional forgery. The theft went unnoticed until a Covenant routine audit detected an anomalous signature in Vienna. The crucifix emits a Corruption signature only when worn. The Vienna spike was the thief testing it: letting himself be hit, thrown down stairs, convinced of his invincibility.

The Compact is brokering the sale. The buyer (revealed late in the case) is Andy Warhol, who has been searching for a guarantee against violence since Valerie Solanas shot him in 1968. The Church learned of the theft through independent means and deployed Cardinal Voss's team. The current bearer (Klaus Reiner, the lead thief) has been wearing the crucifix continuously for two weeks and is entering early-stage immortality mania.

The original meteorite impact site is a lost pagan shrine in the Bohemian Forest, deliberately erased from records by the medieval Church. Countess Martinice, the last surviving member of the family that guarded the crucifix for centuries, holds letters and a family tree that trace the crucifix back to the shrine. If the agents follow this thread, they discover that the meteorite was not a single fragment. There is more iron at the shrine site. Each fragment, if forged into an object and worn, would grant the same immortality property. This changes the classification from "unique relic" to "raw material with replicable properties."

## People

**Cardinal Albrecht Voss.** Church Recovery Lead: Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith. Recover the crucifix. Verify its authenticity in private. Then decide. He is not a villain. He is a sincere man trapped between faith and institutional survival.

**Countess Alžběta Martinice.** Last of the Martinice Line: Keeper of Family Secrets. To pass the story to someone who will believe it before she dies. She is 82. She does not expect to see another winter.

**Klaus Reiner.** Lead Thief: Current Bearer of the Crucifix. Complete the sale, take the money, and disappear. But he is also deeply, compulsively unwilling to remove the crucifix. He will fight to keep it, not for money, but because without it he is mortal again, and he cannot face that.

**Elena Varga.** Compact Broker: The Middlewoman. Close the sale. Collect her 15% commission. Disappear. If the sale fails, pivot to selling information about the buyer to the highest bidder.

**Father Tomas.** Jesuit Historian: Church Recovery Team. Study the crucifix. Publish the truth. That divinity can manifest through meteoric iron as easily as through blessed relics. He sees this as expanding the Church's understanding, not threatening it. He is naive about the institutional consequences.

**Hans (Hansjörg Metzler).** Swiss Guard: Church Recovery Team (Security). Protect Voss. Secondary: recover the crucifix for the Church. If these goals conflict, Voss's safety wins.

**Margot Lefèvre.** Art Dealer: Warhol's Intermediary. Complete the acquisition. Protect Warhol's anonymity. Go home and never take another commission like this.

**Andy Warhol.** The Buyer: Artist, Survivor, Frightened Man. Acquire the crucifix. Wear it. Stop being afraid. He has not thought beyond that moment.

## Places

**Prague Castle Chapel Museum.** A small museum on the castle grounds, housed in a former chapel. Displays objects associated with the Defenestration of 1618 and the Thirty Years' War. Three rooms: the main hall (the defenestration window is here), a side gallery of religious artifacts, and a curatorial office. Quiet, underfunded, staffed by two elderly docents and a part-time curator. The crucifix was displayed in a glass case in the side gallery.

**Countess Martinice's Retirement Home, Brno.** A state-run retirement facility in Brno, two hours east of Prague. Clean, institutional, smelling of boiled cabbage and disinfectant. Countess Alžběta Martinice, 82, occupies room 314. She is sharp, lonely, and has been waiting for someone to ask about the crucifix. Her room contains family letters, a hand-drawn family tree, and a diary entry from 1908 describing a visit to "the old place in the forest."

**Kunstsalon Heller, Vienna First District.** A private antiquities gallery in Vienna's wealthy First District. Discreet, appointment-only, specializing in "special provenance" objects, code for items with questionable ownership histories. The owner,Dieter Heller, is a middleman between thieves and collectors. The back room has a reinforced door, a safe, and soundproofing.

**Bayerische Handelsbank, Munich.** A private bank in central Munich with anonymous safe deposit boxes. Box 718 was rented under a false name three weeks ago. The bank is all marble, brass, and discretion. The vault is underground, time-locked, and guarded.

**Zurich Private Aviation Terminal.** A discreet FBO (fixed-base operator) serving private jets at Zurich Airport. The meeting between Varga and Warhol's intermediary occurred here, in a private lounge. The terminal has flight logs, a café, and staff who are paid to forget faces.

**Pagan Shrine Site, Bohemian Forest (Šumava).** A remote clearing deep in the Šumava National Park, near the West German border. The shrine was demolished in the 11th century, but the foundation stones remain. A circle of weathered granite blocks, half-buried under moss and fallen leaves. The air is cold even in summer. Metal detectors register faint returns beneath the soil. The site has a palpable stillness. No birdsong, no animal tracks. The Corruption signature is low but constant: an ambient hum at the edge of perception.

**Jagdhütte Alpenblick, Bavarian Forest.** A remote hunting lodge 3 km from the West German–Czech border. Stone construction, timber roof, two floors. The main hall has a stone fireplace, mounted antlers, and heavy wooden furniture. The basement is a cold storage room converted to a meeting space. The lodge is owned by a Compact shell company. Isolated, nearest village is 12 km. Access by a single forest road.

## Possible outcomes

### Contain (Covenant Objective)

The agents recover the crucifix before the exchange and deliver it to a Keep vault. The shrine site is mapped and sealed. The Church never gets confirmation of the crucifix's true nature. Warhol returns to New York, unaware of how close he came.

### Deny Use

The agents prevent the sale but cannot recover the crucifix. If the thief is neutralized (immobilized without killing, which is impossible to do with conventional violence), the crucifix can be removed from his neck while he is unconscious or restrained. If the Church recovers it, the agents must intercept before the Vatican takes possession.

### Witness and Report

The exchange happens. Warhol acquires the crucifix. The agents document the transfer and file a containment failure report. The Covenant has to decide whether to attempt a second recovery from a celebrity civilian. A diplomatic and operational nightmare. This is a "case with a scar": the Decay track advances, but the cost is paid.

### Catastrophe

The thief's immortality mania escalates at the exchange. He challenges everyone present to kill him, and they try. Multiple deaths. The crucifix is lost in the chaos. The Compact disappears. The Church covers up its involvement. The Covenant is blamed for a bloodbath on European soil. The Decay track advances by 3.
