# The Barbarian's Cup

## The call

A Covenant watch-list artifact designated "Bodhidharma's Teacup" has surfaced at a black-market auction in Macau. The artifact is a 6th-century stoneware cup, originally from the Shaolin Temple, missing since the Cultural Revolution. That transmutes any liquid contacting its inner surface into a lethal, undetectable toxin. Liquid retains all physical properties: the poison cannot be seen, smelled, or tasted. The cup is being auctioned by a Macau-based antiquities broker. Covenant intelligence has identified at least two confirmed bidders: a foreign extremist organization and a multinational pharmaceutical corporation. The auction window is open. The closing date is unconfirmed but believed to be within 10 days.

## If the team does nothing

The cup has reached a bank vault in Macau. At a private showing, a plant dies and the bidders finally believe the seller. Shi Yánxìn makes contact as the Daewon delegation arrives. The cup’s hum grows loud enough to keep Rosário awake. The auction at the abandoned fort on Coloane is the buyers’ last chance before the cup leaves Macau.

## Behind the incident

Bodhidharma's teacup is not a weapon. It was a tool of enlightenment, brewed by the monk who brought Chan Buddhism to China. For a thousand years, Shaolin monks maintained it with a daily ritual: fill with green tea, let it sit, pour onto the roots of the oldest tree. The tea was "poison to delusion", killing attachment, illusion, the sleep of avoidance. In 1966, the Cultural Revolution reached the monastery. A 14-year-old novice hid the cup in a wall cavity. He spent 8 years in a labor camp. The cup spent 20 years alone. Without the daily ritual, its nature drifted. "Poison to delusion" became simply poison. The cup does not know it changed. It still hums when near liquid. It still thinks it's brewing wakefulness.

Six weeks ago, a looter found the cup in the abandoned monastery and sold it to a Hong Kong antiquities dealer for $200. The dealer tested it on a plant. The plant died in 30 seconds. He contacted João Rosário in Macau, who recognized the cup from a Covenant watch-list he was shown years ago (Rosário has a complicated history with the Covenant, he's been an informant, a target, and a broker, sometimes simultaneously). Rosário organized a blind auction. Three bidders emerged: the Al-Quds Liberation Front (fictional extremist group, $4.2M bid), Daewon Pharmaceutical (Seoul-based, $7M bid, they believe the poison mechanism can be isolated to kill viruses and cancer), and, quietly, through an intermediary: Shi Yánxìn, the now-74-year-old monk who hid the cup in 1966 and has been searching for it since his release from the labor camp.

## People

**João "The Jesuit" Rosário.** Antiquities Broker: Former Seminarian, Current Disaster. Close the auction. Get the cup out of his life. Survive whatever comes next. If survival means betraying the bidders to the Covenant, he's willing, but he needs it to look like it wasn't his choice.

**Farouk Al-Masri.** Cell Leader: Al-Quds Liberation Front. Get out. He doesn't know how. He's hoping, without admitting it. That someone (the agents, the police, fate) will intervene so he doesn't have to make a choice.

**Nasim.** Second-in-Command: The Real Danger. Acquire the cup. Use it. He has identified a reservoir in the Hong Kong New Territories. He has a plan. He believes this is the will of God. He will not be stopped by argument, only by force or superior tactics.

**Dr. Park Min-ho.** CEO: Daewon Pharmaceutical. Acquire the cup. "Understand" it. Use its "gift" to cure disease. He has not considered the possibility that the cup's gift is not cure but death. He will not consider it. The universe does not make mistakes.

**Dr. Yoon Seo-yeon.** Head of R&D: Daewon Pharmaceutical. Understand the mechanism. If the mechanism cannot be controlled, prevent Daewon from releasing anything derived from it. She would rather be a whistleblower than a mass killer, but she's not a hero. She's a scientist trying to do the right thing while keeping her life intact.

**Shi Yánxìn.** The Last Guardian: Shaolin Monk, Age 74. Recover the cup. Restore it if possible. Contain it if not. Tell it he's sorry. Then, if the agents allow, return to Shaolin and plant a new tree where the old one was. The original tree died in 1967. No one knows why. The monk knows.

**Inspector Matias Guterres.** Macau Judiciary Police. Solve the case. If the case turns out to involve things he can't explain, he'll accept that, but he won't accept being lied to. He's a cop, not a soldier. He wants answers, not glory.

**Agent Chen Wei.** Chinese Ministry of State Security. Secure the cup for the People's Republic of China. Prevent it from leaving Chinese territory. Secondary: prevent a diplomatic incident. She will not start a fight, but she will finish one.

## Places

**Banco de Macau, Safety Deposit Vault.** A Portuguese colonial-era bank on Macau's main avenue, Avenida de Almeida Ribeiro. Marble floors, slow ceiling fans, staff who've worked there for 30 years. The cup is in box 419, rented under the name "Silva Import/Export" (Rosário's shell company). The vault is time-locked, accessible during banking hours with the key + manager approval. After hours requires infiltration (Tech Diff 3, Sneak Diff 2). The bank has an old silent alarm system, unreliable but still connected to the Macau police station three blocks away.

**Rosário's Antique Shop, Rua de São Paulo.** A narrow three-story shop near the ruins of St. Paul's: Macau's most famous landmark. Ground floor: legitimate antiques (mostly fake: Rosário is a competent forger). Second floor: Rosário's office, ledger books, auction records, a wall safe behind a framed print of St. Francis Xavier. Third floor: Rosário's apartment, cluttered, bachelor, he's been sleeping on the couch because his bedroom is too close to the safe where he briefly stored the cup and now the room feels "wrong." He can't explain it. The bedroom door has been closed for two weeks.

**The Tea House, Travessa da Paixão.** A traditional Macanese tea house on a narrow side street whose name means "Passion Lane." Bamboo screens, private rooms with sliding paper doors, staff paid to forget faces. The viewing room (Room 7, at the back) still contains the table where the cup was displayed during private viewings for both the Front and Daewon. The potted orchid in the corner is dead. A single drop of water touched the cup's interior during the Front's viewing (Nasim, distracted by the cup's humming, accidentally let it contact a water droplet on the table). The tea house owner is baffled and slightly superstitious about Room 7 now. He's been burning incense in there.

**Daewon Pharmaceutical Regional Office, Hong Kong.** A sleek 23rd-floor office in a Central District tower. Floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Victoria Harbour. The office has a meditation room, kombucha on tap, a living wall of ferns, and a framed quote from Park's favorite wellness book: "The universe provides for those who align." Below the surface: a secure R&D briefing room where Yoon has been analyzing the cup data. Her laptop contains preliminary findings, and copies of data she hasn't shared with Park.

**Al-Quds Safe House, Coloane Village.** A rented fishing cottage on the southern coast of Coloane Island. Isolated, overgrown, smells of salt and neglect. The cottage has two rooms: a front room with maps, a shortwave radio, a laptop (incongruously modern: Farouk's), and a cork board with photographs of Macau infrastructure, and a back room where Nasim sits in the dark with a photograph of the cup. Farouk sleeps in the car. A rented Toyota parked in the weeds. He doesn't like being in the same building as the photograph. The cottage has no running water (the pump is broken). The irony of planning water poisoning from a house with no water is lost on Nasim.

**Camões Garden, Macau.** A quiet public garden named after the Portuguese poet Luís de Camões, whose bronze bust watches over the grotto. Grottoes, banyan trees with aerial roots, mosaic-tiled benches, old men playing Chinese chess. Every morning, an old monk sits on the same bench near the grotto, feeding pigeons from a paper bag. He's been doing this for three weeks, since he arrived in Macau. The pigeons are very fat. The monk is very patient. He chose this bench because it has a clear sightline to both the garden entrance and the back path. He sees everyone who enters.

**Fortaleza de Coloane (Abandoned Portuguese Fort).** A crumbling 17th-century Portuguese fort on Coloane's eastern headland. Cannon emplacements overlooking the South China Sea. The cannons are rusted, pointing at nothing. The central courtyard has been cleared for the auction: a single wooden table (borrowed from a village church), six folding chairs, a portable generator powering a single bare bulb hung from a cannon mount. The fort is isolated. One dirt road in, the sea on three sides, cliffs dropping 30 meters to rocks and white water. Rosário chose it because "no one comes here." The wind is constant. The bulb swings. The sea is loud.

## Possible outcomes

### Contain (Covenant Objective)

Recover the cup before the auction closes. Deliver to a Keep vault. The monk performs the restoration ritual or confirms the quiescence condition. The cup is stored upside-down on a surface untouched by violence. The stone sewn into Shi Yánxìn's robe. The cup goes quiet.

### Deny Use

Prevent any bidder from taking possession. If the auction happens, intercept the handoff. The cup must not leave Macau. The pharma company is reckless, not hostile. They can be outmaneuvered. The terrorist cell is inexperienced. Their leader can be flipped, their second-in-command must be stopped.

### Witness and Report

The auction completes. A bidder takes the cup. The Covenant must track and recover. A much harder operation. The Decay track advances. The cup is in the wind with an owner who intends to use it. This is the "case with a scar" outcome.

### Catastrophe

The cup is used. A water supply is poisoned OR Daewon synthesizes and releases a "treatment" derived from the poison. The Decay track advances by 3. If the catastrophe is a poisoned water supply, thousands die within hours. If it's Daewon's "cure," the death toll is slower but global. A treatment regimen that secretly kills patients over weeks. Either outcome is a campaign-defining failure.
