# Glossary of Game Terms {#chapter-glossary}

Quick reference for all game-specific terms used in the Neon Relic core rules. Entries are alphabetical. Cross-references point to the chapter where the full rules appear.

## A {#21-a}

**Age Group.** Character creation choice determining starting Attribute and Skill point pools. Three tiers: Young (14 attribute / 10 skill), Experienced (13 / 12), Senior (12 / 14). See [Character Creation](#chapter-character-creation).

**Anchor.** A physical object linked to a humanizing memory. Once per session, a character may interact with their Anchor to heal 1d4 Corruption. See [Anchors](#08-anchors).

**Armor Rating.** The number of Gear dice rolled to absorb incoming damage. Each 6 rolled cancels one point of damage. See [Health, Damage & Armor](#chapter-health).

**At-Will.** A talent usage frequency. At-will talents can be activated any number of times, paying the listed Corruption cost for each activation. Passive talents (no activation required) are always at-will. See [Character Creation](#chapter-character-creation).

**Attribute Dice.** White dice in the pool, equal to the relevant Attribute score (Strength, Agility, Wits, or Empathy). See [Core Resolution](#chapter-core-resolution).

## B {#21-b}

**Background.** Recovery Division's equivalent of Departments. Defines the agent's pre-Covenant expertise and grants narrative hooks. See [Divisions](#chapter-divisions).

**Broken.** State when any Attribute reaches 0. The character is incapacitated and rolls on the appropriate Critical Injury table (Physical or Mental). See [Health, Damage & Armor](#chapter-health).

## C {#21-c}

**Case File.** A Covenant mission concerning an artifact and the people affected by it.

**Clearance Level (CL).** Personal equipment authorization tier (1–5). Equals Division base CL plus Age Group modifier (Young −1, Experienced +0, Senior +1), minimum 1. Your CL Pool (equal to your CL) determines how much gear you can requisition during the Equipping Phase via point-buy. CL does not control information access. That is governed by Covenant Standing. See [Requisitioning Gear](#requisitioning-gear).

**Contact.** Someone who can give the agents information, access, or help.

**Containment Truth.** Information necessary for safe resolution of a case, such as a relic's trigger, appetite, tether, shielding method, quiescence condition, or transfer sequence. See [Case Files](#chapter-case-file).

**Corruption.** Cumulative psychological and occult damage track. Gained from pushing rolls, Division Talent activation, Fear, artifact use, and supernatural exposure. **Maximum Corruption Threshold** = 10 + Empathy score; exceeding it causes permanent catatonia and character retirement. See [Corruption, Fear & Healing](#chapter-corruption).

**Covenant Request Packet.** A field request sent to the Covenant for delayed support such as archive pulls, identity checks, freight screening, or comparative case data. Only one packet may be sent per shift, and responses take at least 2 shifts. See [Case Files](#chapter-case-file).

**Critical Injury.** A specific wound or trauma rolled on a D66 table when a character becomes Broken. Physical criticals use the Physical table (STR/AGI = 0); mental criticals use the Mental & Emotional table (WIT/EMP = 0). Some entries are marked † (Death Check). See [Health, Damage & Armor](#chapter-health).

## D {#21-d}

**D66.** A roll of two six-sided dice read as a two-digit number (first die = tens, second = units). Used for Critical Injury tables. Range: 11–66.

**Damage Type.** One of three categories: **Physical** (targets STR or AGI), **Mental** (targets WIT or EMP), or **Corruption** (added directly to the Corruption track). See [Health, Damage & Armor](#chapter-health).

**Death Check.** A single d6 roll required when a Critical Injury is marked †. On 1–2: the character dies (or is permanently retired for mental death). On 3–6: survival, but the Critical Injury still applies. See [Health, Damage & Armor](#chapter-health).

**Department.** Specialization within a Division. Wayfinder agents choose Wings, Keep agents choose Departments, and Recovery agents choose Operational Paradigms instead. Stack is a **Keep department**, not a separate Division. See [Divisions](#chapter-divisions).

**Development Points (DP).** The shared resource the cell earns during Aftermath and spends on Headquarters facilities, personnel, and infrastructure upgrades. See [Headquarters](#chapter-headquarters).

**Difficulty.** The number of successes (6s) required to pass a standard skill roll. Default Difficulty is 1 if a rule does not state otherwise. Opposed rolls compare totals instead of using a fixed Difficulty, and a few special rolls such as Fear checks use their own binary rules. See [Core Resolution](#chapter-core-resolution).

**Director of Agents (DA).** The table’s facilitator role. The DA presents scenes, adjudicates uncertainty, and portrays the world and opposition. See [Director of Agents Guidance](#chapter-gm-guidance).

**Disposition.** An NPC's current openness to engagement in Social Conflict, tracked from 1 (Closed) to 5 (Open). At Disposition 3 or higher, direct low-risk questions are answered without a roll; social rolls are reserved for sensitive disclosures or requests against self-interest. See [Social Conflict](#chapter-social-conflict).

**Division.** Character class within the Verdant Covenant. The three playable Divisions are Wayfinder (research/intelligence), Recovery (field retrieval), and The Keep (vault security, custody, and logistics). See [Divisions](#chapter-divisions).

**Division Item.** A signature supernatural object issued to every member of a Division. Wayfinder: Verdant Codex. Recovery: Verdant Satchel. Keep: Warden's Bracer. See [Divisions](#chapter-divisions).

**Division Talent.** A class-specific ability unique to one Division. Costs +1 to +2 Corruption to activate depending on impact. Usage frequency (at-will, per-session, or per-case-file) determines how often it can be activated. See [Divisions](#chapter-divisions).

**Dying.** State when a Broken character has an active Death Check Critical Injury (result marked †) that has not yet been resolved. An untreated Dying character will die. See [Health, Damage & Armor](#chapter-health).

## E {#21-e}

**Equipping Phase.** A formal between-case step where agents requisition gear from Stack using their CL Pool (point-buy: sum of item CLs ≤ personal CL). Above-CL items require a 1d6 roll (success on 6; Stack agents succeed on 5–6). All requisitioned items are returned at case end. See [Requisitioning Gear](#requisitioning-gear) and [Case Files](#chapter-case-file).

**Experience Points (XP).** The game's only advancement currency. XP are awarded at the end of each session through debrief questions and spent to raise skills, buy new talents, or increase Clearance Level. XP is tracked as three values: **Total XP** (lifetime earned), **XP Spent** (cumulative purchases), and **Current XP** (Total − Spent, available to spend). See [Advancement and Talents](#chapter-advancement).

## F {#21-f}

**Fast Action.** One of three action types in combat. Represents a quick or secondary effort: moving one zone, drawing a weapon, taking cover, or using a small item. See [Combat](#chapter-combat).

**Fear Check.** A special binary Wits roll triggered by supernatural horror. One or more 6s = success. Any 1s = +1 Corruption. No 6s = failure (+1 Corruption + Panic Table roll). See [Corruption, Fear & Healing](#chapter-corruption).

**Fear Rating.** Narrative classification (0–5) of how terrifying a creature or event is. Used by the DA to determine when to call for a Fear check. 0 = no check triggered. See [Corruption, Fear & Healing](#chapter-corruption).

**Free Action.** An action in combat that requires negligible effort: speaking a short sentence, dropping an item, or glancing at surroundings. Unlimited per turn. See [Combat](#chapter-combat).

## G {#21-g}

**Gear Dice.** Black dice in the pool contributed by equipment. Each 1 rolled on a Gear die causes the item to degrade one step. See [Core Resolution](#chapter-core-resolution).

**General Talent.** A talent available to any character regardless of Division, providing a mechanical exception or situational bonus. General Talents are chosen at character creation in place of a Division Talent or purchased later for 6 XP. The canonical list lives in [Advancement and Talents](#chapter-advancement).

## H {#21-h}

**Headquarters (HQ).** The Covenant facility serving as the team's base of operations between Case Files. Provides medical care, equipment requisition via Stack during the Equipping Phase, research facilities, and downtime activities. See [Headquarters](#chapter-headquarters).

## I {#21-i}

**Insight.** A permanent mechanical bonus gained from certain Critical Injuries, marked **(Insight)** in the injury name or effect. Represents a silver lining from trauma, heightened perception, empathy, or expertise. Cannot be removed. See [Health, Damage & Armor](#chapter-health).

## L {#21-l}

**Location.** A place the agents can visit during a mission.

## M {#21-m}

**Maximum Corruption Threshold.** The point at which a character's mind permanently fractures: 10 + Empathy score. Exceeding this value retires the character. See [Corruption, Fear & Healing](#chapter-corruption).

## N {#21-n}

**Named Threat.** A significant antagonist in a Case File, a faction operative, corrupted NPC, or supernatural entity important enough to have a name, motivations, and stat block. See [Case Files](#chapter-case-file).

## O {#21-o}

**Organization.** A group with an interest in the mission, such as a rival faction, the police, or the local press.

## P {#21-p}

**Panic Table.** A d6 table rolled on Fear check failure. Determines the character's involuntary response: Fight (1), Flight (2), Freeze (3), Denial (4), Compulsion (5), or Fugue (6). See [Corruption, Fear & Healing](#chapter-corruption).

**Per-Case-File.** A talent usage frequency. Per-case-file talents have limited uses that reset at the start of each new Case File via Case File Reset. Reserved for the most powerful, case-altering talents. See [Character Creation](#chapter-character-creation).

**Per-Session.** A talent usage frequency. Per-session talents have limited uses that reset at the start of each new play session via Session Reset. Most Medium-impact talents use this frequency. See [Character Creation](#chapter-character-creation).

**Psychoanalyze.** A skill used to read psychological states, detect deception, stabilize distressed people during social scenes, and treat mental and emotional Critical Injuries. See [Social Conflict](#chapter-social-conflict) and [Health, Damage & Armor](#chapter-health).

**Push.** After a roll, reroll the eligible Attribute and Skill Dice once. Keep all sixes and all Gear Dice. A standard push costs 1 Corruption. See [Core Resolution](#chapter-core-resolution).

## S {#21-s}

**Scene.** An encounter in which the agents pursue a lead, face a threat, or make a decision.

**Shift.** The core unit of operational time in a Case File. Each day contains 4 shifts of 6 hours each: Morning, Day, Evening, and Night. See [Case Files](#chapter-case-file).

**Skill Dice.** Green dice in the pool, equal to the relevant Skill rating (0–5). See [Core Resolution](#chapter-core-resolution).

**Slow Action.** One of three action types in combat. Represents a committed effort: attacking, using a skill, administering first aid, or reloading a weapon. See [Combat](#chapter-combat).

**Stunt Points.** Extra successes (6s) beyond the required Difficulty on a roll that allows stunt spending. In combat, they can be spent on special effects such as increased damage, disarming, or knockback. See [Combat](#chapter-combat).

## T {#21-t}

**The Verdant Covenant.** The ancient secret society the player characters serve. Discovers, recovers, and contains supernatural artifacts to protect humanity. Organized into three operational Divisions (Wayfinder, Recovery, The Keep). Stack (Logistics) is a department within The Keep. See [The Verdant Covenant](#01-the-verdant-covenant).

## V {#21-v}

**Verdant Codex.** Wayfinder Division Item. An enchanted field journal that secures memory, assists with language and symbol work, highlights likely historical correlations, and preserves documents. See [Divisions](#chapter-divisions).

**Verdant Satchel.** Recovery Division Item. A specially enchanted field bag that masks minor artifact signatures, stabilizes transport, warns of instability, and keeps basic field tools ready. See [Divisions](#chapter-divisions).

## W {#21-w}

**Warden's Bracer.** Keep Division Item. Dampens nearby emissions during handling, warns of containment failure, enables safer handling of cursed objects, absorbs 1 Corruption per session, and grants +1 Armor Rating. See [Divisions](#chapter-divisions).

## Z {#21-z}

**Zone.** An abstract area of space used for combat positioning, a room, corridor, alley, or rooftop section. Moving one zone costs a Fast Action; moving two zones (running) costs both the Fast and Slow Action for the round. See [Combat](#chapter-combat).
