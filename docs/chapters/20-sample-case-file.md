# The Spear That Went Dark {#chapter-sample-case-file}

::::: pair
:::: column
Buenos Aires, 1987. At the Ferreyra estate a velvet-lined groove runs the length of an empty vault. Someone has tried to wash the blood off the floor. Lucía Ferreyra has kept one thing from the police: her father came down here every evening with a dish of ash.

## Brief the Agents {#20-brief-the-agents}

Three nights ago someone took a spearhead and its restored shaft from the estate. The family insists that the Covenant recover it quietly. At a clinic across town a patient’s old wounds have opened in the shape of a cross. The team has the estate address and Detective Mateo Suárez’s office number. Ask which call they make first.
::::

:::: column
## What Happened {#20-what-happened}

The family kept the Spear of Destiny under an arrangement with the Church. Their vault was a ritual site. The Vantablack Compact financed a theft through intermediaries, but the handoff failed. Each change of hands strengthens the relic. Among frightened people it seeks out a voice accustomed to command. Its chosen bearer retains his own judgment and comes to believe that obedience is his birthright.

## If Nobody Intervenes {#20-if-nobody-intervenes}

The wounded thief seeks help first. Hospital reports of cruciform injuries bring police and reporters to the clinic. The Compact moves its fixer, Iván Luján, into position for a second handoff. As he approaches the Spear, radios fall silent and bystanders repeat his words. Within a fortnight he will present it in public. For hours everyone within earshot will obey him. If the agents disrupt the sale or get the crowd away from Luján, play out what changes.

## At the Shrine {#20-at-the-shrine}

Luján asks a stranger to kneel. The stranger does. Give the agents time to recognize what is happening before the crowd forms. They can separate him from his audience, call on Barral, or try an approach of their own. The Tier 3 Spear carries a d20 Artifact Die and costs 3 Corruption to activate. Fresh blood and a public claim of authority provoke a pulse that becomes a burst. In its zone, listeners obey the claimant. If it fractures, old wounds open across the bearer’s body and nearby electronics go dead.

For safe transport, keep the shrine quiet and dim, clear followers and cameras, and silence declarations of authority. Lay lead-lined cloth over consecrated ash and salt, wrap the Spear, then have the Keep agent lead the recovered suppression rite. The Church knows how to keep the rite going. The Covenant can provide a secure vault. Who will hold the next key?

If the buyer gets away, the port receipt gives the team a destination. If Luján speaks in public, witnesses remember obeying him long after the command fades. Those people may be the next agents’ first callers.
