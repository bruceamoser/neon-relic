# Rival Factions {#chapter-rival-factions}

::::: {.faction emblem="compact" file="01"}
## The Vantablack Compact {#17-the-vantablack-compact}

A discreet transnational conspiracy of fixers, collectors, intelligence cutouts, and black-market brokers who treat artifacts as leverage. The Compact prefers acquisition, quiet coercion, and deniable theft over open war.

Campaign role: **covert competition**.

::: profile
**Early signs**  
Money appears before personnel. Witnesses change stories, shell companies surface, safehouses are cleaned out, and cutouts stop answering phones.

**Pressure pattern**  
Bribe, buy, divert, and isolate. The Compact wants access routes and proof of custody before it wants a firefight.

**Escalation**  
Disposable intermediaries are replaced by disciplined field professionals. Abductions, route closures, and quiet snatch attempts begin.

**Crisis move**  
Final snatch, export handoff, or disappearance of the carrier and all supporting evidence.
:::

::: operative
**Typical Compact Operative**

| STR | AGL | WIT | EMP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 |

**Skills** Sneak 3, Shoot 2, Manipulate 2, Investigate 2

Compact operatives have 1 point of Armor (concealed vest) and carry a suppressed pistol (Ammo Die d6). They do not fight unless trapped.
:::

::: supplement
The Covenant rarely works a case alone. When agents arrive at a scene, someone else is usually already interested, buying access, intimidating witnesses, rerouting artifacts, or maneuvering through official channels. These are **rival factions**: external organizations with their own agendas, assets, and timetables that do not align with safe containment.

## Player-Facing Signs of Rival Pressure {#17-player-facing-signs-of-rival-pressure}

Before a faction makes its move, show at least one sign:

- a witness suddenly has counsel
- a hospital says the file is gone
- the wrong priest arrives first
- a dockmaster becomes unhelpful for no obvious reason
- a car appears twice in one day
- the team's cover story stops being enough

The faction should feel like a tightening web, not a surprise teleport.
:::

Use the Compact when you want professionals who can mirror Covenant tradecraft without matching Covenant ethics.
:::::

## The Ember Accord {#17-the-ember-accord}

A true-believer occult movement built around revelation, purification, and sacrificial faith. The Accord believes artifacts disclose sacred truths that must be activated, witnessed, and survived.

Campaign role: **fanatical escalation**.

::: profile
**Early signs**  
Repeated language, devotional flyers, symbols appearing in mundane spaces, worried families, and witnesses speaking of ordeal as revelation.

**Pressure pattern**  
Gather believers, create witnesses, provoke activation, reinterpret suffering as proof.

**Escalation**  
Cells stop hiding and start arranging audiences, vigils, or rites that make secrecy impossible.

**Crisis move**  
Public activation event or sacrificial rite designed to spread the artifact's logic.
:::

::: operative
**Typical Accord Zealot**

| STR | AGL | WIT | EMP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 2 | 2 | 4 |

**Skills** Manipulate 3, Lore 2, Fight 2

Accord zealots have no armor and rarely carry firearms. They fight desperately when cornered and are immune to Intimidate. A devoted leader (the Herald) has EMP 5, Lore 3, and causes a Corruption Burst (Rating 2) on a first encounter.
:::

Use the Ember Accord when the problem is not only possession of the artifact but an ideology that demands activation regardless of cost.

## Majestic-12 (MJ-12) {#17-majestic-12-mj-12}

A heavily militarized branch of the American Department of Defense responsible for handling anomalous threats. MJ-12 seeks to reverse-engineer artifacts into Cold War weaponry and treats human cost as a logistics problem.

Campaign role: **state power and containment-through-force**.

::: profile
**Early signs**  
Federal credentials where they should not be, restricted files pulled overnight, surveillance vans, sealed streets, and officials suddenly unavailable.

**Pressure pattern**  
Observe, classify, isolate, and then remove. MJ-12 turns open civic space into controlled terrain.

**Escalation**  
Black-bag detention, evidence seizure, roadblocks, and classified transport.

**Crisis move**  
Overwhelming tactical response or forced site lockdown with the artifact still active inside.
:::

::: operative
**Typical MJ-12 Field Agent**

| STR | AGL | WIT | EMP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 3 | 3 | 2 |

**Skills** Shoot 3, Fight 2, Investigate 2, Tech 1

MJ-12 field agents wear concealed ballistic vests (Armor 2) and operate in pairs. They have authority to detain, restrict access, and commandeer resources. They are not equipped for supernatural phenomena and gain Corruption normally from artifact exposure.
:::

::: supplement
**How much does the government actually know?**

The US government's awareness of occult artifacts is fragmented and deliberately compartmentalized. MJ-12 is not a unified policy. It is a black-budget program buried inside the Department of Defense, known to fewer than two dozen officials. Most of the federal government has no idea artifacts exist.

MJ-12 knows artifacts are real and dangerous. They do not understand **why** artifacts exist, what the entities are, or what containment actually requires. Their approach is military: secure the site, extract the object, study it in a controlled facility, and weaponize anything useful. This approach has produced catastrophic failures, classified incidents at research sites that MJ-12 attributes to operator error rather than acknowledging the artifacts' inherent nature.

The Covenant remains independent for three reasons: (1) it predates MJ-12 by centuries and has institutional knowledge that no government program can replicate; (2) the Covenant operates globally across jurisdictions that no single nation controls; and (3) every time a government has attempted to monopolize artifact containment, the results have been worse. The Covenant's archives contain multiple case studies of state-run containment programs that collapsed, leaked, or were corrupted by the artifacts themselves.

MJ-12 and the Covenant are aware of each other. The relationship is hostile but restrained: MJ-12 considers the Covenant an unauthorized non-state actor operating on US soil, and the Covenant considers MJ-12 dangerously incompetent. Neither can afford open conflict. The Cold War provides cover for both: MJ-12 hides its operations behind national security classification, and the Covenant hides behind the assumption that secret societies are conspiracy theories.
:::

Use MJ-12 when the threat is surveillance, black-bag extraction, official deniability, and overwhelming tactical response.
:::::

## The Hellfire Consortium {#17-the-hellfire-consortium}

An elite, global network of corporate executives, politicians, and billionaires who utilize blood magic and ancient rituals for financial gain and political manipulation. They treat the occult as a tool for extreme capitalism.

Campaign role: **elite corruption and patronage**.

::: profile
**Early signs**  
Lawyers, private security, shell purchases, hospitality staff under orders, luxury transport, and polite denials backed by money.

**Pressure pattern**  
Buy the venue, buy the witness, buy the clinic, buy the route, buy the cleanup.

**Escalation**  
Private estates become operational sites, staff become accomplices, and ritual specialists arrive under business cover.

**Crisis move**  
Controlled activation for profit, leverage, or bargaining power in a sealed private environment.
:::

::: operative
**Typical Consortium Operative (Private Security)**

| STR | AGL | WIT | EMP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 |

**Skills** Shoot 3, Fight 3, Manipulate 1

Consortium security wear visible body armor (Armor 3) and operate in teams of four. They enforce exclusion zones without engaging in conversation. Consortium principals (the wealthy clients) are rarely present at operational sites but can get a port closed or a witness transferred with a phone call.
:::

Use the Consortium when the artifact is moving through luxury channels, shell companies, private estates, or buyers who think occult catastrophe is an acceptable line item.
