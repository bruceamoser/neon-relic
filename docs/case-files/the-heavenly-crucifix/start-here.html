The Heavenly Crucifix

Case File for Neon Relic — a complete 8-piece document package for 2–4 sessions.

DA Summary: The Crucifix of St. Vitus — an iron cross that grants immunity to bodily harm — has been stolen from Prague Castle, replaced with a forgery, and is being brokered for sale by the Vantablack Compact. The Catholic Church has deployed a recovery team. The buyer is Andy Warhol. The original meteoric iron, from which the crucifix was forged, still lies beneath a lost pagan shrine in the Bohemian Forest. Three Containment Truths, six organizations, seven locations, eight NPCs, and a three-way standoff at a Bavarian hunting lodge.

Quick Setup

  1. Print the Operations Board and the Organization Reference. These are your dashboard.
  2. Print the Location Pages and NPC Cards. Keep them behind your screen.
  3. Print and cut the Information Cards. Hand them to players when discovered.
  4. Give the players the Case Brief — Player when they receive their assignment.
  5. Start the clock at Day 10, Morning shift.

Pacing

Single session: Start at Day 8. Advance aggressively — 2–3 org squares per shift. The board tightens fast.

Multi-session: Start at Day 10. Session 1: Prague and Brno (the theft, the countess). Session 2: Vienna, Munich (the trail). Session 3: Bohemian Forest, Bavaria (the confrontation). Session 4: aftermath and the shrine site.
