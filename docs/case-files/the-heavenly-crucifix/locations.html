Location Pages — The Heavenly Crucifix

Seven locations. Pull the relevant page when agents arrive.

L1 — Prague Castle Chapel Museum L1

DescriptionA small museum on the castle grounds, housed in a former chapel. Displays objects associated with the Defenestration of 1618 and the Thirty Years' War. Three rooms: the main hall (the defenestration window is here), a side gallery of religious artifacts, and a curatorial office. Quiet, underfunded, staffed by two elderly docents and a part-time curator. The crucifix was displayed in a glass case in the side gallery.
AvailabilityDay shifts only (Morning, Day). Closed Evening and Night. Public access — agents can enter as tourists.
NPCs PresentCurator Marek (civilian, not a full NPC — cooperative, knows the forgery is good but not who made it). After O3M1: Cardinal Voss visits, may overlap with agents.
InformationI1 (the forgery details), I2 (theft timeline), I3 (police report). I4 (shrine site mention) if agents ask specifically about the crucifix's origin.
OrganizationsNone present at case start. O5 after M1 — police presence.
+ Positive: Curator Marek is flattered by attention. A successful Manipulate (Diff 1) or genuine interest in the history earns full cooperation. The forgery can be examined — Tech or Lore (Diff 2) reveals the iron composition is wrong (modern alloy, not meteoric).
− Negative: If agents are aggressive or overtly "official," Marek becomes nervous and calls security. Police arrive next shift. If agents handle the forgery without gloves, they leave fingerprints that O5 traces (M1 triggers one day earlier).

L2 — Countess Martinice's Retirement Home, Brno L2

DescriptionA state-run retirement facility in Brno, two hours east of Prague. Clean, institutional, smelling of boiled cabbage and disinfectant. Countess Alžběta Martinice, 82, occupies room 314. She is sharp, lonely, and has been waiting for someone to ask about the crucifix. Her room contains family letters, a hand-drawn family tree, and a diary entry from 1908 describing a visit to "the old place in the forest."
AvailabilityDay and Evening shifts. Visiting hours end at 8 PM. Staff are indifferent — agents can pass as relatives.
NPCs PresentNPC2 (Countess Martinice). After O3M1: Father Tomas (NPC5) may also be visiting — he arrived ahead of the agents.
InformationI4 (family tree — traces crucifix to shrine), I5 (the shrine location), I6 (the family letters — mentions "iron from the sky"). I7 (the 1908 diary) if agents build rapport.
OrganizationsNone.
+ Positive: The Countess is eager to help. She gives the agents her family letters and tree if treated with respect. If agents mention the Church, she becomes guarded — she knows the Church suppressed the shrine story and does not trust clergy. A Psychoanalyze (Diff 1) reveals she's been holding onto this story her whole life, waiting for someone who would believe her.
− Negative: If agents are dismissive, impatient, or threaten the Countess, she closes down permanently. Information can only be recovered via HQ fallback (losing 2 shifts). If Father Tomas is present, he will report the agents' visit to Cardinal Voss immediately.

L3 — Kunstsalon Heller, Vienna First District L3

DescriptionA private antiquities gallery in Vienna's wealthy First District. Discreet, appointment-only, specializing in "special provenance" objects — code for items with questionable ownership histories. The owner,Dieter Heller, is a middleman between thieves and collectors. The back room has a reinforced door, a safe, and soundproofing.
AvailabilityDay and Evening shifts. Appointments required — agents must pose as buyers or use credentials. Night access requires infiltration.
NPCs PresentDieter Heller (civilian, not a full NPC — nervous, greedy, easily intimidated). Elena Varga (NPC4) may be present depending on O2 progression.
InformationI8 (the Vienna test — Heller witnessed it), I9 (Compact's involvement), I10 (buyer is "an American, very famous — you would know the name"). I11 (Munich bank vault location) if Heller is pressured or paid.
OrganizationsO2 (Compact). After O2M2: O6 may begin surveillance.
+ Positive: Heller is terrified of the thief after watching him survive three point-blank gunshots in the back room. He will cooperate with anyone who seems capable of making "this nightmare end." A Manipulate or Command (Diff 1) gets full disclosure. Heller can arrange a meeting with Varga.
− Negative: If agents arrive aggressively, Heller triggers a silent alarm. Compact security arrives in 1 shift. If the meeting goes public (police called, gunfire), O5 advances by 2 squares.

InformationI11 (Varga's contact), I12 (Reiner's psychological state — the shirt and note suggest testing), I13 (Compact's fee structure — found in a discarded envelope). I14 (the Zurich meeting) from a travel itinerary in the box.

− Negative: A botched infiltration triggers the bank's security. Munich police respond. O5 advances by 1. The box is impounded as evidence — agents must request it through HQ, costing 2 shifts.

L6 — Pagan Shrine Site, Bohemian Forest (Šumava) L6

DescriptionA remote clearing deep in the Šumava National Park, near the West German border. The shrine was demolished in the 11th century, but the foundation stones remain — a circle of weathered granite blocks, half-buried under moss and fallen leaves. The air is cold even in summer. Metal detectors register faint returns beneath the soil. The site has a palpable stillness — no birdsong, no animal tracks. The Corruption signature is low but constant: an ambient hum at the edge of perception.
AvailabilityAll shifts. Accessible only by hiking trail (2 shifts from Prague, 1 shift from the hunting lodge). No roads. No facilities. Agents must carry in equipment.
NPCs PresentNone at case start. After O6M1: a two-man KGB surveillance team is in the area (not at the shrine itself — observing from a distance).
InformationI18 (the meteorite fragments — still in the ground), I19 (the pagan origin confirmed — Slavic ritual objects among the stones), CT-1 (full confirmation of the pagan origin), CT-3 (additional iron fragments are harvestable).
OrganizationsAfter O6M1: Soviet Intelligence is active in the area.
+ Positive: Excavating a fragment (Force + Tech combined, Diff 2, 2 hours) recovers a fist-sized piece of meteoric iron. A Lore roll (Diff 2) confirms its composition matches the crucifix. Agents can seal the site with a Containment Protocol ward (NPC5's talent if present, or the team's Keep agent).
− Negative: Digging without proper containment training triggers a Corruption Aura: +1 Corruption per scene at the site. If the KGB team is present, they photograph the agents and report to Moscow. O6 advances by 1. If agents extract iron without authorization, they are in Covenant protocol violation — Standing loss on case completion.

Isolated — nearest village is 12 km. Access by a single forest road.

Warhol is in the parking lot. Any gunfire triggers O5 (police) and potentially O6 (Soviet response). A Breach result here is catastrophic — multiple deaths, international incident, artifact lost.
