Case Brief — Operation Crucible

Case Designation:Operation Crucible Region:Central Europe (CSSR, Austria, BRD) Classification:Secret — CL 2+ Issuing Division:Wayfinder — Research Wing Date:[Current Date]

Situation

An artifact designated "The Crucifix of St. Vitus" (see attached Relic Summary) has been stolen from the Prague Castle chapel museum. The theft was not discovered by local authorities — a forgery was substituted. A Covenant routine audit detected an anomalous Corruption signature in Vienna, triggering this case. The artifact is an iron crucifix historically associated with Count Jaroslav Bořita of Martinice, who survived the 1618 Defenestration of Prague. Covenant analysis suggests the survival was not miraculous — the crucifix possesses properties that render its wearer immune to bodily harm.

Objectives

  1. Primary: Locate and recover the Crucifix of St. Vitus. Contain in a Keep vault under standard Tier 2 protocols.
  2. Secondary: Identify the theft crew and the party or parties brokering or bidding on the artifact.
  3. Tertiary: Determine whether additional fragments of the source material exist and, if so, secure or seal the origin site.

Initial Leads

  1. Prague Castle Chapel Museum (L1): The theft site. The forgery is still on display. Examine the forgery for craftsmanship clues. Interview museum staff about the substitution timeline.
  2. Countess Martinice, Brno (L2): Last surviving descendant of the Martinice family. The crucifix was in her family for generations before being donated. She may know its history — and its origin.
  3. Vienna First District (L3): The Corruption signature spiked at an antiquities gallery for 90 minutes. Someone was testing the artifact there.
  4. Munich (L4): The signature was steady for three days at a bank vault. The crucifix was stored there between showings.

Operational Parameters

  • Rules of Engagement: Standard artifact recovery. Minimize civilian exposure. The artifact's bearer cannot be harmed by physical force — do not attempt lethal engagement. Containment requires removal of the crucifix from the bearer's person.
  • Church Protocol: Cardinal Voss's team operates under ecclesiastical authority. Do not engage them as hostile unless they initiate. Do not share Covenant intelligence. They are a parallel operation, not an allied one.
  • Compact Protocol: The Vantablack Compact is a criminal organization. Interdiction of Compact operatives is authorized but not at the expense of the primary objective.
  • Shrine Site: If investigation reveals the origin site of the crucifix's source material, report coordinates to HQ immediately. Do not extract material without authorization.
