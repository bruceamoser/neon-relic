Organization Reference — The Heavenly Crucifix

DA reference: milestone descriptions with cross-link instructions. Read when a milestone square is reached on the Operations Board.

O1 — The Thieves (Starting Value: 8 → 6 squares open)

Professional theft crew. Lead: Klaus Reiner (NPC3). Currently in possession of the crucifix. Motivated by payment; unaware of the full implications.

O1M1 (Day 5): Reiner tests the crucifix in Vienna. The signature spikes. Reiner becomes fully aware of his invincibility. He begins demanding a higher price. → Advance O2 (Compact) by 1 square. Reiner becomes reckless in subsequent scenes — +1 die on any Intimidation attempts he makes.
O1M2 (Day 3): Immortality mania fully sets in. Reiner engages in overtly dangerous behavior — picking fights, challenging people to shoot him. The Compact becomes alarmed. → Advance O3 (Church) by 1 square (the Church detects the reckless behavior). I15 becomes available.
O1M3 (Day 2): Reiner agrees to the exchange location. The hunting lodge is set. → Advance O2 by 1 square (Compact finalizes arrangements). Activate O4 (The Buyer) — Warhol's flight is booked.

O2 — Vantablack Compact (Starting Value: 9 → 7 squares open)

Brokered by Elena Varga (NPC4). The Compact does not want the crucifix — they want the commission. Playing all sides.

O2M1 (Day 7): Varga secures the buyer's commitment. The Compact begins moving money through Zurich accounts. → I8 becomes available. Advance O5 (Police) by 1 square (financial activity flags an Interpol alert).
O2M2 (Day 5): Varga learns the Church is closing in. She doubles her fee and begins preparing an exit strategy. → I12 becomes available. NPC4's disposition changes: she is now willing to betray the buyer if the agents offer a better deal.
O2M3 (Day 2): Exchange location finalized: hunting lodge in the Bavarian Forest. Varga arranges security. → L7 becomes accessible. Advance O3 by 1 square (Church intercepts the location).

O3 — The Church / Cardinal Voss (Starting Value: 10 → 8 squares open)

Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith recovery team. Lead: Cardinal Voss (NPC1). Team: Father Tomas (NPC5), Hans (NPC6).

O3M1 (Day 7): Church team arrives in Prague. Cardinal Voss visits the Castle, confirms the forgery, and begins his own investigation. He will approach the agents if they are present — cordial, non-hostile, but non-cooperative. → NPC1, NPC5, NPC6 are now present at relevant Locations. I5 becomes available.
O3M2 (Day 5): Voss learns of the Compact's involvement and the buyer's existence. He escalates: the Church team is now authorized to use "extraordinary measures." → NPC6 (Hans) will physically intervene if agents approach the crucifix. I13 becomes available.
O3M3 (Day 2): Voss learns the exchange location. The Church team moves to Bavaria. Voss believes he can talk the thief down — he is wrong. → NPC1 is present at L7. If agents share CT-1 with Voss before this point, he pauses operations for 24 hours (inner conflict).

O4 — The Buyer / Andy Warhol (DORMANT — Starting Value: 8 → 8 squares)

Dormant until triggered by O1M3 or agent investigation. Once active, squares pre-fill from Day 8.

O4M1 (Day 5): Warhol's intermediary confirms the purchase. The Gulfstream is fueled. Warhol tells his Factory assistant he's "going to Europe for a very important meeting." → I17 becomes available. NPC7 (the intermediary) can be identified.
O4M2 (Day 2): Warhol arrives at Zurich airport. He is nervous, quiet, carrying a Polaroid camera and a checkbook. He asks the intermediary if the thing "really works." → NPC8 (Warhol) is present at the exchange. I21 becomes available.

O5 — Prague Police (Starting Value: 11 → 9 squares open)

Inspector Radim Svoboda leads the theft investigation. Not Covenant. Not compromised. Methodical.

O5M1 (Day 6): The forgery is discovered by museum staff, who report the theft. Svoboda opens an investigation. → I3 becomes available. L1 has increased police presence — Sneak or Manipulate rolls required for covert access.
O5M2 (Day 3): Svoboda traces the theft to the Compact's Vienna operation. He requests international cooperation. → Advance O2 by 1 square (Compact feels heat). I19 becomes available.

O6 — Soviet Intelligence (DORMANT — Starting Value: 6 → 6 squares)

Dormant until triggered by: (a) agents accessing the shrine site L6, or (b) O2M1 if agents fail a Delayed action to cover financial tracks. The KGB has been monitoring Compact money movements in Vienna for years.

O6M1 (Day 4): A KGB Vienna station officer identifies the transaction pattern and connects it to the Prague theft. A two-man surveillance team is deployed. → I20 becomes available. Agents may notice they are being followed (Investigate Diff 2).
