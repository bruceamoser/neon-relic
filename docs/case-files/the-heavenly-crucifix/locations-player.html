L1 — Prague Castle Chapel Museum, Hradčany L1

PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT
A small museum housed in a former chapel on the Prague Castle grounds. The museum displays objects associated with the Defenestration of 1618 and the Thirty Years' War. The facility consists of three rooms: a main hall containing the defenestration window, a side gallery of religious artifacts, and a curatorial office. The museum is described in public records as underfunded and staffed by two elderly docents and a part-time curator. An iron crucifix of interest was reportedly displayed in a glass case in the side gallery prior to recent events. Current status of the display is unconfirmed. The museum is accessible to the public during standard castle hours.
Accessibility: Day shifts only. Public access — enter as tourists. Closed Evening and Night. Museum staff: limited, likely cooperative if engaged appropriately.
Assessment: Site of confirmed interest. The display case and museum records may contain information about the object and the timeline of events. Source: public records, tourist information, satellite imagery.

L6 — Remote Clearing, Šumava National Park (Bohemian Forest) L6

PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT
A remote forest clearing deep within the Šumava National Park, near the West German border. The site is accessible only by hiking trail — no roads, no facilities. Historical records reference a pre-Christian religious site at this approximate location, demolished in the 11th century. Foundation stones are reported to remain at the site — weathered granite blocks, partially buried under moss and fallen leaves. The site is described in regional folklore as "cold even in summer." Preliminary environmental assessment notes an absence of typical forest wildlife activity in the immediate vicinity — no birdsong, no animal tracks. Metal detection surveys have not been conducted. The site's isolation and limited access make it suitable for discreet visitation but logistically demanding.
Accessibility: All shifts. Accessible by hiking trail only — approximately 2 shifts travel from Prague, 1 shift from the nearest lodging. No roads, no facilities, no lighting. Agents must carry in all equipment. Exercise standard wilderness precautions.
Assessment: Site of high potential interest. Historical association with pre-Christian metallurgy may be relevant to the object's material origin. Geological survey recommended. Source: historical records, regional folklore, terrain analysis, satellite imagery.
