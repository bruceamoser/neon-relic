The Boudica Pact

Case File for Neon Relic — a complete 9-piece document package for 2–3 sessions.

DA Summary: A Tier 2 binding — sustained by a single gold coin buried in a Staffordshire field since the 7th century — has been breached by a farmer who ploughed up the keystone and then vanished. After Boudica's defeat in 61 CE, her dying rage was adopted by something older — a pre-Celtic presence in the earth — and became a creeping blight. The Romans couldn't destroy it. They bargained with it: gold in the earth, the curse sleeps. The Anglo-Saxons reinforced the binding. Now the coin is gone, the farmer is missing, and the binding is failing. The agents must trace the pact from a Mercian monk's chronicle in London to a Roman ledger in the Vatican to a museum vault in Birmingham before the entity the coin was holding back finishes waking up.

Quick Setup

  1. Print the Operations Board and the Organization Reference. These are your dashboard.
  2. Print the Location Pages and NPC Cards. Keep them behind your screen.
  3. Print and cut the Information Cards. Hand them to players when discovered.
  4. Give the players the Case Brief — Player when they receive their assignment.
  5. Start the clock at Day 7, Morning shift.

Pacing

Single session: Start at Day 4. Advance aggressively — 2–3 org squares per shift. Focus on Staffordshire, the chronicle, and the museum infiltration. Skip Rome or handle it via HQ fallback.

Multi-session (2–3): Start at Day 7. Session 1: Staffordshire and London (the farm, the pub, Eadwine's chronicle). Session 2: Rome (the Vatican archive, the ledger). Session 3: Birmingham and Staffordshire (the museum vault, the re-burial).
