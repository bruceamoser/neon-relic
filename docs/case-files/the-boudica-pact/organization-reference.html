Organization Reference — The Boudica Pact

DA reference: milestone descriptions with cross-link instructions. Read when a milestone square is reached on the Operations Board.

O1 — Birmingham Museum (Starting Value: 5 — 5 squares open)

Holds the Staffordshire Hoard — 3,500 pieces of Anglo-Saxon gold and garnet. Curator Dr. Helen Price has spent 10 years studying the hoard. She does not believe in cursed coins. She has, however, been having headaches since the gallery was rearranged last month — right around when Arthur Dunn pulled the keystone coin from the earth.

O1M1 (Day 4): Dr. Price notices items in the Anglo-Saxon gallery have shifted overnight — a few millimetres, but all in the same direction. She attributes it to cleaning staff. She increases security — additional patrol checks, a review of display case locks. → Museum access becomes harder (Sneak +1 Difficulty). I9 becomes available — "Shifted Displays."
O1M2 (Day 2): Mr. Okonkwo, the night security guard, files a report: the coins in the Anglo-Saxon gallery are "glowing" — a faint reddish warmth visible only in peripheral vision. His supervisor dismisses it as "reflection from the exit sign." Okonkwo knows it's not. He decides to help anyone who takes him seriously. → NPC3 becomes an ally. I15 becomes available — "Okonkwo's Testimony." Okonkwo provides patrol schedules, security gaps, and a 90-second window when the vault camera loops.

O2 — Vatican Archive (Starting Value: 6 — 6 squares open)

Holds Codex Mercaturae Britanniae — a Roman trade ledger mis-catalogued as a medieval merchant's book. Father Matteo, the archivist, has read it. He knows it describes something that shouldn't be in a trade ledger. He's been waiting, quietly, for someone to ask about it.

O2M1 (Day 5): Agents arrive in Rome. Father Matteo confirms the ledger exists but access requires authorization — the appointment system has a six-week backlog. A Manipulate (Diff 2) or genuine scholarly interest expedites access. Church credentials bypass the backlog entirely. → I11 becomes available — "The Book of Reckonings." If agents fail the roll, access is delayed by 1 day — advance the Relic Milestone by 1 day (the entity gets closer to waking).
O2M2 (Day 3): The ledger is accessed. It contains the pact terms in bureaucratic Latin: "One binding, site CXVII, province of Britannia. Payment: gold, measured by weight. Condition: gold in earth, binding holds. Gold removed, subject returns. Gold returned, binding restored." No quantity specified — a single coin is sufficient. The Romans were precise about loopholes. → I12, I13 become available. CT-1 (trigger condition) and CT-3 (quiescence condition) sources unlocked. I21 also becomes available — the ledger's provenance.

O3 — Ministry of Agriculture (DORMANT — Starting Value: 6 — 6 squares)

Dormant until: (a) blight radius exceeds 5 miles, (b) a civilian reports the insect behavior to authorities, or (c) Day 3 (automatic activation — the blight is too visible to ignore). Once active, squares fill from the activation day forward. Before activation, all squares remain blank — the Ministry hasn't noticed anything yet.

O3M1 (Day 3): The Ministry declares an "Agricultural Health Zone" in Staffordshire. Roadblocks appear on county roads. Quarantine checkpoints are staffed by officials who don't know what they're containing. Mr. Philip Croft, an agricultural inspector, is assigned to investigate. → Movement within the county is restricted. Agents must use credentials or Sneak to move freely. I17 becomes available — "Quarantine." NPC7 (Croft) is now active in the area.
O3M2 (Day 1): The quarantine expands. Birmingham is placed on alert. The museum increases security further — additional guards, more frequent patrols. The re-burial site (L7) is inside the quarantine zone. → Museum infiltration is now Hard difficulty (+1 additional Difficulty on all Sneak/Tech rolls). The re-burial must be conducted covertly. I20 becomes available — "Quarantine Expands."

O4 — The Farmer's Trail (DORMANT — Starting Value: 4 — 4 squares)

Dormant until agents actively search for Arthur Dunn. Dunn is not hiding — he's lost. The coin is calling him back to the farm, and he's been circling Staffordshire at a 20-mile radius, getting closer each day, unable to go home and unable to leave.

O4M1 (Day 4): Agents trace Dunn to a fishing lodge in Scotland. The lodge owner says Dunn arrived, stayed two nights, and left abruptly. "Said he couldn't sleep. Said something was calling him. Paid in cash and drove off toward the border." → I14 becomes available — "The Anomaly Report" from Dr. Rhys (the timing aligns — agents can connect Dunn's disappearance to the coin). Dunn's trail now leads south, back toward Staffordshire.
O4M2 (Day 2): Dunn is found — or his trail goes permanently cold. If found: he's in a motel near Lichfield, 20 miles from his farm. He's been there for three days. He hasn't slept. He keeps touching his coat pocket. "I can still feel it," he says. "It's not in my pocket anymore. I threw it in a river in Scotland. But I can still feel it." The coin is unrecoverable — it's in a Scottish river. The agents confirm they need a museum coin. → I18 becomes available — "Dunn Found." NPC6 (Arthur Dunn) is now accessible.

Blank Organization Rows (O5–O8)

O5–O8 are blank rows on the Operations Board. The DA may use them for organizations that emerge during play — local police, a Covenant rapid-response team, a journalist investigating the blight, or a rival faction. No milestones are pre-written. Fill in as needed.
