O1 — Birmingham Museum and Art Gallery

Status: ACTIVE (Unaware)

Public Profile: A major regional museum holding the Staffordshire Hoard — approximately 3,500 pieces of Anglo-Saxon gold, garnet, and silver recovered from a site near Lichfield in 1984. The hoard's excavation site is within the geographical area of current interest. The museum is a public institution governed by a board of trustees, funded through public grants and admission revenue. It employs civilian academic, curatorial, and security staff. The collection is legally protected under UK heritage legislation. The museum has no known connection to any activity beyond its public mandate of conservation, research, and display. Its staff are not security-cleared and are unaware of any investigation.

Known Activity: Standard museum operations — public exhibition, academic research, conservation. No unusual activity reported. The museum's Anglo-Saxon gallery curator (Dr. Helen Price) and night security staff (Mr. Samuel Okonkwo) have been identified as potential sources of information should engagement become necessary.

Assessment: The museum is a civilian institution — not an adversary. Its collection may contain items of contextual relevance to the current anomalies. Diplomatic or academic engagement is the preferred approach. The museum's legal protections and public accountability mean any attempt to access or remove collection items would face significant legal obstacles.
