Case Brief — Operation Earthbound

Case Designation:Operation Earthbound Region:Staffordshire, England Classification:Secret — CL 2+ Issuing Division:Wayfinder — Research Wing Date:[Current Date]

Situation

A Covenant Wayfinder monitoring station detected an anomalous Corruption signature in rural Staffordshire. The signature pattern is consistent with a breached supernatural binding — ancient, Roman-era in origin. The epicenter is a farm. The farmer, one Arthur Dunn, is missing.

Local residents report unusual insect behavior, deteriorating water quality, and a spreading "quiet zone" in which ambient sound is perceptibly dampened. The signature is strengthening. HQ analysis suggests a containment pact of considerable age has been compromised. The binding's exact nature, the entity bound, and the mechanism of the pact are unknown at this time.

Objectives

  1. Primary: Identify the binding mechanism and determine what was bound. Restore containment before full breach.
  2. Secondary: Locate the missing farmer and determine his connection to the breach.
  3. Tertiary: Secure any surviving documentation related to the original binding for Covenant archives.

Initial Leads

  1. Dunn's Farm, Staffordshire (L1): The epicenter. Site examination may reveal the binding locus and evidence of what was disturbed. Exercise caution — the site emits Corruption.
  2. The Plough and Furrow Pub (L2): Arthur Dunn's local. Patrons may have seen what he found or know where he went. Someone in the village knows more than they're saying — or less.
  3. Birmingham Museum and Art Gallery (L3): The museum's Anglo-Saxon collection includes pieces recovered from the region. Curatorial records may identify items catalogued as anomalous, unrecovered, or inconsistently documented.
  4. The British Library, London (L4): Manuscript holdings include early medieval chronicles. A search for references to pacts, bindings, or local traditions in the Staffordshire region during the Anglo-Saxon period may yield relevant information.

Operational Parameters

  • Rules of Engagement: Standard containment with modification — the objective is restoration, not seizure. The binding is likely location-specific. Do not attempt to relocate or extract binding components without analysis confirming it is safe to do so.
  • Entity Protocol: The bound entity cannot be fought, reasoned with, or bargained with. Do not attempt direct confrontation. Containment must be achieved through binding restoration, not force.
  • Auric Component: Covenant analysis of the Corruption signature suggests an auric (gold) component to the binding mechanism. Any gold artifacts recovered from the epicenter region should be treated as potential binding elements. Do not remove gold from the region without authorization.
  • Corruption Hazard: The binding locus emits ambient Corruption. Rotate agent exposure. Standard field decontamination protocols apply. Report any Corruption symptoms immediately.
  • Museum Protocol: The Birmingham Museum's collection is legally protected. Any acquisition of collection items will be contested. The Covenant prefers diplomatic resolution. Escalation requires Watch Commander authorization.
