NPC8 — The Entity (The Red Woman / Boudica's Curse)

RoleBound Presence — Antagonist as Condition, Not Character
OrganizationNone
DispositionN/A — Not a person. A wanting. A condition of the landscape.
SecretThe entity is not Boudica. It's older. It was in the earth before the Celts, before the Beaker people, before anyone. It had no name, no form, no voice. Boudica's dying curse gave it all three. It took her rage because rage was something it understood — the desire to take back what was lost. It has been slowly becoming Boudica for 1,900 years. It's not there yet. But it's closer than it was. The "Red Woman" the villagers speak of — the figure at the edge of fields — is the entity wearing Boudica's shape. It is learning to be human-shaped. It is a slow learner. But it has had 1,900 years of practice.
GoalBe. Take back. The entity doesn't want to destroy. It wants to occupy. It wants the land returned to a state before human agriculture, before human settlement, before human presence. It is not evil. It is incompatible. It does not hate humans. It does not understand humans. It understands wanting. Its wanting is toxic to living things — not out of malice, but because what it wants and what life requires cannot coexist.
Artifact ConnectionBound by the gold. The gold is the only thing it cannot consume. Gold is inedible — even to a pre-Celtic earth-presence. The pact exploits this: "Here is what you cannot take. As long as it remains, neither can you." The entity does not understand contract law. It understands that when the gold is in the earth, it cannot move. The Romans understood this. They were precise about loopholes.
Starting KnowledgeNone (the entity does not communicate directly)
Gained KnowledgeExperienced through symptoms — locusts, brine, silence, breathing earth, the voice at Day 1. CT-2 is learned by observing what the entity is drawn to.
LocationsL1, L7 (the binding locus — its prison and its door). Present as an atmospheric condition throughout the blight radius. At Day 1: partially manifest at L7 as a heat-shimmer and a voice.
+ / − ResultsThe entity cannot be fought, reasoned with, or bargained with. It can only be bound. The binding words are not a negotiation. They are a reminder: "The debt is paid. The gold is placed. The binding holds." The entity hates the words. They cause it pain — not physical, but existential. The words assert a truth the entity cannot accept: that it can be denied. At the climax: The entity resists re-binding. The agent performing the ritual feels its attention — a pressure at the base of the skull, a voice in a language that predates human speech, a wanting so intense it feels like gravity. The +2 Corruption is the cost of standing at the threshold and closing the door while something ancient screams on the other side.
