Case Brief — Operation Undersleep

Case Designation:Operation Undersleep Region:Cormsil, Yorkshire Dales, England Classification:Secret — CL 2+ Issuing Division:Wayfinder — Research Wing Date:[Current Date]

Situation

Covenant Wayfinder detected an occult signature beneath a small farming town in the Yorkshire Dales. The signature is ancient, pre-Roman, and recently awakened. Pattern analysis suggests a subterranean presence — an entity bound to a specific geographic location, previously dormant, now active.

Simultaneously, the town of Cormsil experienced a library fire three weeks ago with 25 casualties. However, all 25 casualties are reportedly alive and residing in the town. Local accounts describe the victims "running into the woods" during the fire and returning the next day with no memory of the intervening night.

The Covenant considers these events likely connected. Agents are dispatched to investigate the signature's source, determine the connection to the fire, and assess the threat.

Objectives

  1. Primary: Identify the source of the occult signature and determine its nature.
  2. Secondary: Investigate the library fire and the anomalous survival of the 25 casualties.
  3. Tertiary: Determine whether the entity can be contained or neutralized.

Initial Leads

  1. Cormsil Village (L1): Interview residents, visit the burned library, establish the timeline. The town is small — everyone knows what happened, and most will talk if approached right.
  2. The 25 Survivors (L1, L4): Locate and interview. All are residing in Cormsil. Preliminary reports suggest unusual behavior — agents should observe before engaging directly.
  3. St. Cuthbert's Parish Church (L3): May hold historical records. The town dates to the medieval period, and the church is Norman, built on Saxon foundations. The current vicar may be an ally.
  4. Regional Libraries and Archives: If the entity is pre-Roman, historical texts may reference it. Bodleian Library (Oxford, L5) and British Library (London, L6) are potential sources.

Operational Parameters

  • Rules of Engagement: The entity is presumed location-bound. Do not attempt direct confrontation — nature and capabilities are unknown. The 25 survivors are to be treated as civilians until their status is confirmed.
  • Community Protocol: Cormsil is a small community — approximately 400 residents. Agents should maintain a low profile. The town is divided between those who call the survival a miracle and those who are uneasy. Agents should avoid taking sides publicly.
  • Evacuation Contingency: If the entity proves hostile or expansive, prioritize civilian evacuation. Cormsil has one access road. Coordinate with local authorities only if necessary — and only after the supernatural element is confirmed beyond reasonable doubt.
  • Historical Research: If the entity is pre-Roman, standard field investigation may be insufficient. Agents are authorized to access academic archives at Oxford (Bodleian) and London (British Library). Reader's cards can be arranged through Covenant academic affiliates.
