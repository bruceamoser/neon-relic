NPC6 — Shi Yánxìn

RoleThe Last Guardian — Shaolin Monk, Age 74
OrganizationO4 — The Monk
Disposition4 — Patient, kind, quietly funny
SecretThe monk blames himself for everything. He was 14 when the Red Guards came. He hid the cup instead of fleeing with it. He chose the cup over his own safety. He spent 8 years in a labor camp, telling no one what he'd hidden. When he was released in 1974, the monastery was gutted, the wall was intact, the cavity was empty. Someone else had found it. He's been searching for 13 years. He found the Hong Kong dealer six months ago, but the cup had already moved to Rosário. He arrived in Macau three weeks before the agents. He's been waiting in the park, feeding pigeons, composing the apology he'll give the cup when he finally holds it again. The apology is 13 years long.
GoalRecover the cup. Restore it if possible. Contain it if not. Tell it he's sorry. Then — if the agents allow — return to Shaolin and plant a new tree where the old one was. The original tree died in 1967. No one knows why. The monk knows.
Artifact ConnectionLast person to hold the cup before it was hidden. He was 14. He remembers it humming — not cold and menacing like now, but warm, like a cat purring against your palm. He's been listening for that sound for 38 years. The stone from Bodhidharma's cave is sewn into the lining of his robe — he's carried it since his release from the labor camp, waiting for the day he'd need it.

Gained KnowledgeI18 (O4M2), CT-3
