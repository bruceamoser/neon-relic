Anomalous Characteristics

Preliminary reports describe the object as consistently cold to the touch — below ambient temperature regardless of environmental conditions. This characteristic is anomalous for ceramic materials, which typically equilibrate with ambient temperature. The thermal anomaly has not been instrument-verified.

The object's physical integrity is noted: a visible crack runs from rim to base, yet the vessel has not fragmented. For a ceramic object of apparent age, this structural stability despite visible damage is unusual. The crack appears to be a single impact fracture with no propagation — ceramic materials with pre-existing cracks typically continue to fracture under thermal stress or handling. The object's resistance to further cracking is anomalous but may be explainable by material composition (analysis pending).

Additional anomalous characteristics may exist but have not been observed or reported. Physical examination is required for comprehensive assessment.
