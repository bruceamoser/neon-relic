Case Brief — Operation Teahouse

Case Designation:Operation Teahouse Region:Macau (Portuguese administration) Classification:Secret — CL 2+ Issuing Division:Wayfinder — Research Wing Date:[Current Date]

Situation

A Covenant watch-list artifact designated "Bodhidharma's Teacup" (see attached Relic Summary) has surfaced at a black-market auction in Macau. The artifact is a 6th-century stoneware cup — originally from the Shaolin Temple, missing since the Cultural Revolution — that transmutes any liquid contacting its inner surface into a lethal, undetectable toxin. Liquid retains all physical properties: the poison cannot be seen, smelled, or tasted. The cup is being auctioned by a Macau-based antiquities broker. Covenant intelligence has identified at least two confirmed bidders: a foreign extremist organization and a multinational pharmaceutical corporation. The auction window is open. The closing date is unconfirmed but believed to be within 10 days.

Objectives

  1. Primary: Locate and recover the cup before the auction closes. Contain in a Keep vault under standard Tier 2 protocols.
  2. Secondary: Identify all bidding parties and prevent any from taking possession.
  3. Tertiary: Determine whether the cup can be safely neutralized and whether any surviving guardians from Shaolin can be located.

Initial Leads

  1. Macau Bank Safety Deposit Vault (L1): The cup is believed to be stored here between viewings. The box is rented under a shell company. Confirm the location and assess access options.
  2. Broker's Antique Shop, Rua de São Paulo (L2): Front for the auction operation. Legitimate business upstairs. The broker has auction records and a wall safe.
  3. Tea House, Travessa da Paixão (L3): Reported site of private viewings for potential bidders. The cup was shown here recently.
  4. Public Gardens and Temples, Macau (L4): If a guardian monk exists, these are the places to look. Religious sites and public gathering spaces throughout the peninsula.

Operational Parameters

  • Rules of Engagement: Standard artifact recovery with extreme caution. Handle the cup only by its outer surface, with gloves. The inner surface must not touch any liquid — including sweat, condensation, or rain. If the inner surface contacts liquid, that liquid becomes lethal.
  • Bidder Protocol: Do not engage bidders as hostile unless they initiate. The pharmaceutical company is reckless but not known to be violent. The extremist group's capabilities are unknown.
  • Jurisdictional Restriction: Do not share Covenant intelligence with Portuguese or Chinese authorities. Macau's jurisdictional status is sensitive. Any leak could trigger a sovereignty incident.
  • Time Sensitivity: If the auction completes and a bidder takes possession, recovery becomes significantly harder. The priority is preventing handoff.
